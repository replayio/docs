Connect Replay QA to your Obvious workspace by installing the Replay QA connector and authorizing your Replay QA account with OAuth.

<Steps>

## Open Workspace Connectors

Sign in to Obvious and open [Workspace Connectors](https://app.obvious.ai/settings/workspace/connectors).

Find the **Replay QA** connector card. If it is not visible, use the search field to search for **Replay QA**.

<Figure

alt="The Replay QA connector card in Obvious Workspace Connectors, with its Connect button visible."

src="/images/replay-qa-obvious-connectors.png"

height={976}

width={1315}

/>

## Click Connect

On the **Replay QA** connector card, click **Connect**.

## Authorize Replay QA with OAuth

Follow the OAuth prompts to sign in to the Replay QA account you want to connect and authorize Obvious to use it. When authorization completes, Obvious finishes connecting the Replay QA connector to your workspace.

## Ask Replay QA to test your app

In the Obvious chat, ask: `Use the Replay QA mcp server to test this app`.

<Figure

alt="An Obvious chat asks Replay QA to test the app, with the Replay QA project dashboard open beside it."

src="/images/replay-qa-obvious-mcp-test.png"

height={1768}

width={3072}

/>

## Watch the QA run in Obvious

Replay QA embeds an iframe on the right-hand side of Obvious. Use it to watch the QA run's progress as it tests your app.

<Figure

alt="The Replay QA dashboard embedded on the right-hand side of Obvious, showing the app recording and QA results."

src="/images/replay-qa-obvious-run-progress.png"

height={1760}

width={3072}

/>

</Steps>
